I improved the Size-exclusion chromatography toolkit (SECAT) by about 40% through optimizations and multiprocessing support for non-parametric Loess normalization.

The work is in the upstream SECAT repository as a pull request: [grosenberger/secat#2](https://github.com/grosenberger/secat/pull/2).

The method is described in the STAR Protocols paper [Network-centric analysis of co-fractionated protein complex profiles using SECAT](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xpro.2023.102293).
